About designer

designer is a collaborative platform built for all creative professionals to showcase their work, connect with peers, and engage with the global design community. Our mission is to empower designers by providing a modern, visually stunning space to present collections, share technical details, and highlight creative journeys.

At AIOAK designer portal, designers from every field—including fashion, automotive, arts, UX/UI, graphic, and interior design—can maintain professional portfolios, collaborate on projects, and participate in exclusive events. We welcome creativity in all forms and support designers in building their brand and reaching new audiences.

We believe in the power of collaboration. designer enables designers to network, collaborate on new projects, and participate in exclusive events. By bringing together diverse talents and perspectives, we foster innovation and elevate the art of design across industries.

Whether you're an emerging designer, an established brand, or a creative enthusiast, designer is your hub for inspiration, partnership, and growth. Join us in shaping the future of design through creativity and collaboration.

I’m Manya Reddy Marri, a fashion professional and graduate of Arizona State University (ASU), with a degree in Fashion. Currently, I work as a Production Manager at Emerald Sundae in Los Angeles, California, where I help manage the full product lifecycle — from design development and sourcing to production, quality assurance, and delivery.

With a background that bridges both design and production, I bring a unique perspective that balances creativity, craftsmanship, and operational efficiency. My journey in fashion has been fueled by innovation, attention to detail, and a passion for translating concepts into high-quality, market-ready garments.

Experience

Vision

I aim to collaborate with forward-thinking brands, designers, and creatives who value innovation, sustainability, and craftsmanship in fashion.
