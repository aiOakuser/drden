A. {{ company_name }} — Privacy Policy (CCPA + CalOPPA Compliant)

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ company_name }} (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and secures personal information across our experiences, including {{ service_brands|join:", " }}, and any other platforms or services we operate.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Name and contact information (email, phone number).
  • Uploaded designs, photos, and full portfolio materials.
  • Messages, consultations, and chat content shared with our team or designers.
  • Payment details that are securely processed by our third-party gateways.

Automatic Information

  • IP address, device data, browser type, and operating system details.
  • Cookies, session identifiers, and usage analytics that describe how you interact with the platform.
  • Approximate location data derived from IP information to help localize services.

User-Generated Content

  • Designer profiles and brand bios.
  • Tailoring measurements and project requirements.
  • Design uploads, inspiration boards, and creative briefs.
  • Consultation notes, requests, and ongoing collaboration history.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Creating, authenticating, and managing user accounts.
  • Providing designer consultations and coordinating tailoring services.
  • Improving features, personalization, and accessibility across our products.
  • Sending platform updates, alerts, marketing promotions, and service notices.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Complying with applicable legal, tax, and regulatory requirements.

3. Designer Messaging & Platform-Only Communication

Designer-to-designer messaging is a premium feature with strict terms. To protect the community and platform integrity, we prohibit sharing personal contact information (email, phone, social media, website URLs) between designers through the platform. We monitor messages to enforce these rules. Violations may result in account suspension. Please review our Terms of Service for full details.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We only share data with:

  • Payment processors that manage transactions on our behalf.
  • Hosting providers, customer-support platforms, and analytics vendors.
  • Designer and tailoring partners when a project requires collaboration.
  • Legal authorities when compelled or when needed to protect safety and rights.

5. California Consumer Rights (CCPA)

California residents may exercise the following rights:

  • Know what categories of data we collect and why.
  • Request a copy of the personal information we maintain.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Opt out of data sharing that is considered a “sale” under CCPA.
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated personal information.

Submit requests by emailing {{ contact_email }}. We will verify your request and respond within the statutory timeframe.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We rely on cookies and similar technologies for authentication, remembering preferences, CalOPPA-compliant analytics, and session management. You can disable cookies in your browser, but doing so may limit certain platform features.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: Data in transit is protected by TLS/SSL; sensitive data at rest is encrypted.
  • Access controls: Role-based access, least-privilege principles, and regular access reviews.
  • Infrastructure: Secure hosting, monitoring, and intrusion detection.
  • Incident response: We have procedures to detect, contain, and remediate security incidents, and to notify affected users when required by law.

No system is 100% secure. We continually assess and improve our defenses. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and relevant regulators as required by applicable law.

Your Security Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account credentials. Use a strong, unique password and do not share your account with others. Immediately notify us at {{ contact_email }} if you suspect unauthorized access. See our Security Policy for full details.

8. Privacy Policy Changes

We will update this policy periodically to reflect new features, regulations, or security practices. When changes occur, the “Last updated” date will be revised and continued use of our services means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Email {{ contact_email }} for privacy questions, CCPA/CalOPPA requests, or accessibility assistance. You can also reach us at {{ company_location }}.
