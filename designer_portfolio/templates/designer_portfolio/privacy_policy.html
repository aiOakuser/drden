A. {{ company_name }} — Privacy Policy (CCPA + CalOPPA Compliant)

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ company_name }} (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and secures personal information across every AIOAK experience, including {{ service_brands|join:", " }}, and any other platforms or services we operate.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Name and contact information (email, phone number).
  • Uploaded designs, photos, and full portfolio materials.
  • Messages, consultations, and chat content shared with our team or designers.
  • Payment details that are securely processed by our third-party gateways.

Automatic Information

  • IP address, device data, browser type, and operating system details.

User-Generated Content

  • Designer profiles and brand bios.
  • Tailoring measurements and project requirements.

  • Consultation notes, requests, and ongoing collaboration history.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Creating, authenticating, and managing user accounts.
  • Providing designer consultations and coordinating tailoring services.
  • Improving features, personalization, and accessibility across our products.
  • Sending platform updates, alerts, marketing promotions, and service notices.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Complying with applicable legal, tax, and regulatory requirements.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We only share data with:

  • Payment processors that manage transactions on our behalf.
  • Hosting providers, customer-support platforms, and analytics vendors.
  • Designer and tailoring partners when a project requires collaboration.
  • Legal authorities when compelled or when needed to protect safety and rights.

4. California Consumer Rights (CCPA)

California residents may exercise the following rights:

  • Know what categories of data we collect and why.
  • Request a copy of the personal information we maintain.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Opt out of data sharing that is considered a “sale” under CCPA.
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated personal information.

Submit requests by emailing {{ contact_email }}. We will verify your request and respond within the statutory timeframe.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We rely on cookies and similar technologies for authentication, remembering preferences, CalOPPA-compliant analytics, and session management. You can disable cookies in your browser, but doing so may limit certain platform features.

6. Data Security

We use encryption, access controls, monitoring, and secure infrastructure partners to safeguard your information. No system is 100% secure, but we continually test and improve our defenses.

7. Privacy Policy Changes

We will update this policy periodically to reflect new features, regulations, or security practices. When changes occur, the “Last updated” date will be revised and continued use of our services means you accept the updated policy.
