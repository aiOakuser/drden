Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ company_name }} (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit globaldesignerhub.com or use any of our products, including the designer dashboard, portfolio pages, and Google OAuth login.

Sharing & Disclosure

We only share personal information with:

  • Vetted service providers (hosting, analytics, payment processors) that support our platform.

We do not sell personal data.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and measure product usage. You can manage cookies directly in your browser settings; disabling cookies may limit certain features.

Data Retention & Security

We retain account data as long as needed to deliver the service or as required by law. We apply administrative, technical, and physical safeguards; however, no online platform can guarantee absolute security, so please use strong passwords and protect your devices.

  • Request account deletion by contacting us at {{ contact_email }}.

Children
