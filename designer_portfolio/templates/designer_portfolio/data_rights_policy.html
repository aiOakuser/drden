E. {{ company_name }} — Data Collection, Use & Deletion Policy (CCPA Notice)

This notice summarizes how {{ company_name }} handles personal information for California residents and describes the controls available to every user of {{ service_brands|join:", " }}.

Your Rights

  • Request deletion of personal information we maintain about you.
  • Request copies of specific data we collected, including how it is used.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Opt out of data sharing that is treated as a “sale” under the CCPA.

How to Request Data Deletion

Send an email to {{ contact_email }} and:

  • Use the subject line “CCPA Data Deletion Request.”
  • Describe the scope of the request (delete all data, specific projects, etc.).
  • Include any relevant usernames, project IDs, or booking references.

Verification

To protect your privacy we may require:

  • The account email address and phone number on file.
  • Proof of identity (such as a code sent to your registered email or phone).
  • Additional documentation if you are an authorized agent submitting the request.

Once verified, we will complete most requests within 45 days, or notify you if more time is needed.
