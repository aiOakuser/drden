NYFW February 14 Runway Highlights

New York Fashion Week (NYFW) for the Fall/Winter 2026 season will take place from Wednesday, February 11, through Monday, February 16, 2026, featuring over 60 runway shows and presentations. The official schedule includes major brands like Tory Burch, Michael Kors, Khaite, and Proenza Schouler, with events centering on the latest American collections.

Key highlights and details for NYFW February 2026 include:

  • Dates: February 11-16, 2026.
  • Major Shows: Proenza Schouler (Feb 11), Tory Burch (Feb 11), Michael Kors (Feb 12), Sergio Hudson (Feb 13), Khaite (Feb 14), and LaQuan Smith (Feb 14).
  • New/Returning Designers: Area (new), along with returning brands such as Kallmeyer, Altuzarra, Fforme, Public School, Zankov, and Aknvas.
  • Location/Tickets: While many shows are private, official public tickets can be found for select events, such as those at Sony Hall through Runway 7 Fashion.
  • Schedule & Information: The Council of Fashion Designers of America (CFDA) manages the official schedule.

The preliminary schedule indicates a strong, eclectic lineup with a mix of established luxury houses and contemporary designers showcasing in NYC.
